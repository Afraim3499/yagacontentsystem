### Updated Operational Directives
1. **3-Batch Staggered Delivery:** Content dispatches in 3 equal batches (Batch 1: Immediate, Batch 2: +30m, Batch 3: +60m) giving 1/3rd of tasks to *every creator* in each batch.
2. **Simplified Completion:** Employees simply press **[Mark as Done]**. Public link submission is removed.
3. **Webhook Response Decoupling:** Instant 200 OK webhook response + HTML/Markdown text auto-escaping helper.
