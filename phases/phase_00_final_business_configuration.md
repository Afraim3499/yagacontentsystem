# Phase 00 — Final Business Configuration

## 1. Objective & Scope
Finalize all foundational business definitions, creator profiles, target platforms, platform posting rules, and operational guidelines prior to file construction.

---

## 2. Configuration Action Items
- Finalize initial list of creators and assigned internal titles.
- Finalize target social platforms (`PL-X`, `PL-CMC`, `PL-TG`, `PL-LINKEDIN`, `PL-INSTAGRAM`, `PL-FACEBOOK`, `PL-BINANCE`, `PL-TRADINGVIEW`, `PL-MEDIUM`, `PL-SUBSTACK`).
- Define daily posting cadences and article frequencies per platform.
- Establish daily recurring engagement requirements per platform.
- Define username naming options (Option 1, Option 2, Option 3) per platform.
- Establish profile disclosure lines and Yaga affiliation standards.
- Designate primary Telegram channels/destination links.
- Compile initial target account follow lists (minimum 5 per platform category).
- Draft voice profiles (tone, sentence length, vocabulary style, humor, storytelling style) for each creator.

---

## 3. Completion Requirement
Every creator-platform combination is clearly defined in business documentation.
