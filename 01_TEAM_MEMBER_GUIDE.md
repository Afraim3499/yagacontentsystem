```

---

## 🚀 STEP 1: User Onboarding

1. Message **@yagacontentbot** on Telegram or click [t.me/yagacontentbot](https://t.me/yagacontentbot).
2. Type `/registration` (or `/start`).
3. When prompted, reply with your display name (e.g. `Alex Crypto` or `Elena Trades`).
4. You will receive your Creator ID (e.g. `CR-002`). Step 1 is complete!

---

## 🌐 STEP 2: Platform Account Onboarding

Connect the social media accounts assigned to you (X/Twitter, Telegram, Medium, Binance Square, Substack, LinkedIn).

1. Type `/onboard` (or tap **[Setup My Platforms]**).
2. Tap **[🔑 Submit Account Details]** and send:
   - **Login Identifier:** Email, phone, or username used for the account.
   - **Password:** Account password *(Saved securely in encrypted database vault)*.
   - **Public Handle:** Your handle (e.g. `@alex_crypto_trades`).
3. Your account will switch to **Active & Posting Ready**.

---

## 📌 STEP 3: Receiving & Posting Daily Content

Daily content is dispatched to your Telegram chat across **3 staggered batches**:
- **Batch 1:** 11:00 AM EST
- **Batch 2:** 11:30 AM EST
- **Batch 3:** 12:00 PM EST

📝 YOUR CONTENT TO POST:
──────────────────────

Here is what you need to know about the next liquidity level...
──────────────────────

🔗 Asset: [View Graphic]

[ ✅ Mark as Done ]   [ 🚨 Report a Problem ]
```

### Action Steps:
1. Copy the customized text and post to your platform.
2. Immediately return to Telegram and tap **[✅ Mark as Done]**.

---

## ⏰ Post Window & Helpful Reminders

- **Goal:** Aim to post within **60 minutes** of dispatch so market content stays relevant.
- **30-Minute Nudge:** If a task remains unposted after 30 minutes, the bot sends a gentle reminder.
- **Need Assistance?** If you face any delay, technical glitch, or broken link, simply tap **[🚨 Report a Problem]** on the task card. Our team is notified instantly and will reply to assist you.

---

## 🚨 How to Report an Issue

1. Tap **[🚨 Report a Problem]** below any task card.
2. A ticket is created in the CRM Issue Desk.
3. System Owners receive an alert and will send a response directly to your Telegram chat.
