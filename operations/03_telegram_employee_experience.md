# Telegram Employee Experience & Bot Menus

## 1. Daily Telegram Tasks & Simplified Completion Flow

### Individual Task Delivery (Batch Message)
```text
YAGA POSTING TASK (Batch 1 of 3)

Platform:
LinkedIn

Account:
[Creator Account]

CAPTION

[Complete creator-specific caption]

IMAGE

[Image appears here or Drive link]

INSTRUCTION

Use the assigned account.
Keep paragraph spacing.
Review caption before publishing.

[Open Account]
[Open Image]
[Mark as Done]
[Report a Problem]
```

---

### Simplified Task Completion Sequence
1. Employee receives batch task message in Telegram.
2. Employee publishes content on platform.
3. Employee presses **[Mark as Done]**.
4. System immediately marks assignment as `Completed`, updates completion stats, and notifies employee:
   `"Task marked as completed! Batch 2 will arrive in 30 minutes."`
5. **No link or proof URL submission is required.**
